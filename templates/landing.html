{# ── Features ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────── #}

Everything you need for image-based PGC analysis

From upload to results, PGCView handles the full pipeline so you can focus on the science.

Flexible upload

Upload images directly from your computer or pick them from your Google Drive. Batch upload dozens of images at once.

Automated inference

Images are preprocessed and sent to a dedicated inference API. Results are saved and surfaced directly in your dashboard.

Job dashboard

Track every submission in a live dashboard. See pending, processing, and completed jobs with full result history.

Access control

Controlled access via Google sign-in with administrator approval. Only vetted researchers can submit jobs.

Per-image results

Results are stored per-image with status tracking, so you always know exactly which images have been analyzed.

Google Drive sync

Already storing images in Google Drive? Pick them directly without re-uploading. Your Drive account connects automatically.
