PGCView

PGCView is an image analysis platform built to support automated detection and segmentation of field images for perennial groundcover (PGC) research — tracking markers and quadrats, classifying ground cover, and surfacing results for researchers.

{# ── Funding & attribution ──────────────────────────────────────────── #}

Funding & Attribution

PGCView was developed for and is supported by RegenPGC, Regenerating America's Working Landscapes to Enhance Natural Resources and Public Goods through Perennial Groundcover — a USDA-funded Coordinated Agricultural Project led by Iowa State University, with partner institutions University of Wisconsin–Madison, University of Missouri, and University of Kentucky.

RegenPGC is supported by Agriculture and Food Research Initiative Competitive Grant No. 2021-68012-35923 from the USDA National Institute of Food and Agriculture.
